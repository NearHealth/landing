FAQ

Common questions

I can't sign in, or I didn't get my verification code

Check that you're using the email address or phone number your account was set up with, and look in your spam or junk folder for the code. You can reset your password from the sign-in screen. If you still can't get in, email us and we'll help.

My plan, claims, or benefits information looks wrong

Plan, claims, and benefits details come from your health plan. Email us with what looks incorrect and we'll check it with your plan.

How do I request an appointment?

Ask in the App's chat. Tell the assistant what kind of care you need and the reason for your visit, and our care team will follow up to arrange it.

Does the AI assistant give medical advice?

No. The assistant helps with plan and administrative questions such as coverage, claims, benefits, and finding care. It does not diagnose conditions. For medical advice, talk to a licensed health professional.

How do I delete my account?

Follow the steps on our Delete Your Account page. You don't need the App installed to make the request.
