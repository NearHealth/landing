Delete Your Account

Near — the NearHealth member app (Android package com.nearhealth.app and iOS)

Last updated: August 22, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your account and the personal data associated with it for the Near app, published on Google Play and the App Store by NearHealth.

Anyone with a Near account can make this request — you do not need the App installed, and there is no cost. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

How to request deletion of your account and data

  1. Write an email to privacy@near.health from the email address registered on your Near account.
  2. Use the subject line “Delete my account”. You do not need to explain why.
  3. We verify that the request comes from the account owner — we may reply to your email to confirm before proceeding.
  4. Once verified, we permanently delete your account and associated personal data within 30 days and send you a confirmation email when it is done.

Email privacy@near.health

What data is deleted, and what is kept

When your deletion request is completed, the following applies:

DataWhat happensRetention period
Account and profile data (name, date of birth, email, phone, postal address, member ID, group ID, password) Permanently deleted Within 30 days of your verified request
Chat history and messages, including health questions you asked Permanently deleted Within 30 days of your verified request
Files and photos you uploaded in the chat Permanently deleted Within 30 days of your verified request
Appointment requests Permanently deleted Within 30 days of your verified request
Claims and health-plan records we must keep for your health plan or by law (for example insurance, HIPAA, and record-keeping obligations) Kept temporarily, then deleted or de-identified Only as long as the legal or contractual obligation lasts
Diagnostic and security logs Kept temporarily, then deleted or de-identified A limited period for troubleshooting and security
Chat history stored locally on your device Removed by you — delete the App or clear its data Until you delete the App or clear its data

Data kept under a legal or health-plan obligation is no longer used to provide you the service and is deleted or de-identified as soon as that obligation ends.

If you don't want to delete your whole account

You can also ask us to delete or correct specific data without closing your account, or request a copy of the personal data we hold about you. Email privacy@near.health from the email address on your account and tell us what you need.
