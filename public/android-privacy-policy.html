Privacy Policy

Near — the NearHealth member app (Android package com.nearhealth.app and iOS)

Effective date: August 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NearHealth (“NearHealth”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use the Near mobile application (the “App”). The App helps health-plan members understand their coverage, review claims and benefits, find nearby care, and request appointments through a chat experience supported by an AI assistant and our care team.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

At a glance

  • We collect the information needed to run your member account: your identity and contact details, your health-plan enrollment data, and the messages, files, and requests you send in the chat.
  • Your chat messages are answered by an AI assistant powered by models we host on our own infrastructure and, when needed, by members of our care team who can view your conversation to help you.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and the App contains no advertising and no third-party analytics SDKs.
  • Camera, photos, microphone, location, and biometric features are all optional and used only when you invoke them.
  • You can request access to your data or deletion of your account and data at any time at privacy@near.health.

1. Information we collect

Account and enrollment information

When your health plan invites you to NearHealth (for example through a CareLink activation link) and you set up your account, we collect and maintain: your first and last name, date of birth, email address, phone number, postal address, member ID, group ID, your health plan and insurer details, your account password (stored in hashed form), and verification codes sent to your email or phone during sign-up and password reset.

Health information you provide

The App is a health-care support tool, so the content you send may include health information: questions you type or dictate in the chat (for example about symptoms, benefits, medications, or providers), appointment requests (care type, visit type, and your reason for the visit), and feedback you submit.

Insurance, claims, and benefits data

To answer your questions, we process information about your coverage received from or on behalf of your health plan: policy and plan details, benefits and coverage summaries, plan documents, claims and claim status, and your care activity history.

Files and photos you choose to share

You may attach files or photos in the chat (for example a bill, a claim document, or your insurance ID card) using your camera, photo library, or file picker — only when you choose to. Text may be extracted from images you share (optical character recognition) on your device and on our servers so the assistant and our care team can read the document and help you.

Voice input

If you use voice input, your device's operating-system speech-recognition service (provided by Apple or Google and subject to their privacy policies) converts your speech to text; we receive the resulting text you send. The App can also read messages aloud using on-device text-to-speech. The microphone is used only while you actively use these features.

Location (optional)

If you use the “Find a Provider” feature and grant location permission, we use your device location to center the map and send your approximate coordinates to our servers to return clinics, doctors, pharmacies, and labs near you. We do not collect location in the background, and the feature works with a manually chosen area if you decline the permission.

Biometric sign-in (optional)

You may unlock the App with Face ID, Touch ID, or fingerprint. Biometric matching happens entirely on your device through the operating system; we never receive, store, or have access to your biometric data — the App only receives a signal that authentication succeeded or failed.

Device and diagnostic data

To keep the App reliable and secure, the App sends diagnostic logs to our servers, which may include device model, operating-system version, app version, error and crash details, and timestamps of technical events. The App does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Marketing attribution (with your permission)

On iOS, the App may ask for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt to understand how you found us online; if you decline, the App works exactly the same, and you can change your choice at any time in your device settings. The Android version of the App does not perform this tracking and does not collect the Android Advertising ID.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide the service: answer your questions about coverage, benefits, claims, and providers; show your plan documents; and maintain your chat history across your devices.
  • To generate assistant replies: your messages, shared files, and relevant plan context are processed by AI language models (see Section 3) and, when needed, reviewed by our care team.
  • To fulfill your requests: when you ask to book an appointment, we create and track the request and share the necessary details so care can be arranged.
  • To secure and improve the App: authenticate you, prevent fraud and abuse, diagnose and fix technical problems, and improve reliability.
  • To communicate with you: send verification codes, respond to your support requests and feedback, and provide service-related notices.
  • To comply with law: meet our legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations.

We do not use your personal information for third-party advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

3. AI assistant and human review

Chat replies in the App are generated by artificial-intelligence language models that we host and run on our own infrastructure. To generate a reply, the content of your conversation and the plan context needed to answer (for example your benefits or a claim you asked about) is processed on our own servers.

Members of the NearHealth care team may view your conversation and, where appropriate, respond directly — for example to complete an appointment request or to help when the assistant cannot. Care-team access is limited to authorized personnel who need it to support you.

The assistant provides plan and administrative support; it does not provide medical diagnoses. Always call your local emergency number in an emergency.

5. How we protect your information

  • All data is transmitted between the App and our servers over encrypted connections (TLS).
  • Your chat history stored on your device is encrypted at rest, with encryption keys kept in your device's secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore).
  • Sign-in credentials and session tokens are held in secure platform storage, and you can add a biometric lock to the App.
  • Access to personal data by our staff is restricted to those who need it to support you, and our systems use access controls and logging.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information using appropriate technical and organizational measures and will notify you and regulators of a data breach where the law requires it.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • Account, enrollment, and health information is retained while your account is active and afterwards only as long as needed to comply with legal, regulatory, insurance, and record-keeping obligations.
  • Chat history is retained so your conversation stays available across sessions; local history on your device is deleted when you delete the App or clear its data.
  • Diagnostic logs are kept for a limited period for troubleshooting and security, then deleted or de-identified.

How to delete your account and data

You can request deletion of your NearHealth account and the personal data associated with it at any time, without needing the App installed:

  • Email privacy@near.health from the email address on your account with the subject “Delete my account”.
  • After verifying the request, we permanently delete your account and associated personal data — profile, chat history, uploaded files, and appointment requests — within 30 days.
  • Some records may be kept longer where the law or our obligations to your health plan require it (for example claims records); those are deleted or de-identified once that obligation ends.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Permissions: camera, photos, microphone, location, and biometrics are optional; you can grant or revoke each at any time in your device settings, and the core chat works without them.
  • Tracking: you can decline or disable the iOS tracking permission at any time in Settings; this never affects App functionality.
  • Access, correction, deletion, and portability: you may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct inaccurate data, or ask us to delete your account and data (see How to delete your account and data) by emailing privacy@near.health from the email address on your account. We respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.
  • Additional rights may apply depending on where you live (for example under U.S. state privacy laws or the GDPR), including the right to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority. We do not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

8. Health information and HIPAA

Some information handled in the App may be protected health information (“PHI”) under the U.S. Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) when we process it on behalf of your health plan. Where HIPAA applies, we handle PHI in accordance with agreements that meet HIPAA requirements, and your health plan's Notice of Privacy Practices also describes your rights over that information.

9. Children's privacy

The App is intended for adult health-plan members and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. Information about covered dependents is managed through the responsible adult member's plan enrollment. If you believe a child has provided us personal information directly, contact us and we will delete it.

10. International processing

We are based in the United States, and our service providers may process data in the United States or other countries. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you through the App or by email before the changes take effect, and we will update the effective date above. Your continued use of the App after the effective date means the updated policy applies.
